# Milestone 14 — Warm Paper & Fountain-Pen Ink Visual Redesign + Jargon-Free Copy Pass

Parent: [`milestones.md`](../milestones.md)

## Objective

Replace the stock Material seed palette with a warm, high-craft visual identity (**Warm Paper, Deep Fountain-Pen Ink & Golden Citrine**) and replace all academic/research jargon and internal requirement IDs in the UI with clear, approachable everyday language (`A-01`, `A-02`).

## Exit criteria

1. `lib/app/theme.dart` implements custom Light (`#FAF7F2` warm paper, `#1C2430` deep fountain-pen ink, `#D97706` / `#F59E0B` golden citrine accents) and Dark (`#11161F` deep slate-obsidian, `#1A2230` elevated ink surface, `#F3F0E8` parchment text, `#FBBF24` citrine gold accents) themes.
2. Zero internal requirement codes (`(B-12)`, `(D-19)`, `(E-08)`, `(A-06)`, etc.) appear in user-facing buttons, headers, subtitles, or tooltips.
3. Unfamiliar research/academic terminology is replaced with self-explanatory everyday wording:
   - `"Hard Landscape"` → `"Schedule & Events"`
   - `"Carryover Triage"` → `"Unfinished from Earlier"`
   - `"Rule of 1 (Floor Day)"` → `"Low-Energy Day (Just 1 Thing)"`
   - `"Zombie Projects"` → `"Stalled Projects (Needs a Next Step)"`
   - `"Gollwitzer If-Then"` → `"When will you start? (After..., I will...)"`
   - `"Horizons & Review"` → `"Plan & Review"`
4. `flutter analyze` and all unit/widget tests pass.
