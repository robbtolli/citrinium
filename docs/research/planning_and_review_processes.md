# Planning & Review Processes for Different Cadences

**Goal:** Provide concrete, step‑by‑step routines people use when they sit down to **plan** or **review** their work/life at various intervals. The steps are extracted from well‑known productivity systems and include rough time estimates and typical tools. All items are linked directly to the source material (clickable Markdown links) as you requested.

---

## 📅 Daily
### Planning (5‑15 min) – *Ivy Lee Method* 
1. **Write down 6 tasks** you will tackle tomorrow.  
2. **Rank them** by true importance (1 = most important).  
3. **Start with #1** when you arrive; ignore everything else until it is done.  
4. **Proceed sequentially** through the list, one task at a time.  
5. **Carry over** any unfinished items to the next day’s list (still capped at 6).  
   - Source: [Ivy Lee method explained](https://todoist.com/productivity-methods/ivy-lee) 

### Review (5‑10 min) – *Cal Newport Daily Shutdown* 
1. **Capture loose ends** – move any stray notes or ideas into your master task system.  
2. **Review your master list** for missing items and set due dates if needed.  
3. **Check the next‑day calendar** for upcoming meetings or deadlines.  
4. **Adjust the week’s plan** based on today’s progress.  
5. **Close down** your workspace and say the shutdown phrase (e.g., *“Shutdown complete.”*).  
   - Source: [Newport shutdown routine](https://focusbox.io/articles/daily-shutdown)

## 📆 Weekly
### Planning & Review – *Getting Things Done (GTD) Weekly Review* (30‑60 min) 
1. **Collect & clear all inboxes** (physical papers, email, voice‑mail, digital capture tools).  
2. **Empty your head** – do a mind‑sweep of any lingering ideas.  
3. **Process the inbox** – decide next actions or file as reference.  
4. **Review action lists** – mark completed items, delete outdated ones, add next actions.  
5. **Review past calendar** – note any follow‑ups from recent meetings.  
6. **Review future calendar** – capture actions triggered by upcoming events.  
7. **Review waiting‑for list** – follow up on delegated tasks.  
8. **Review projects** – ensure every project has at least one next action.  
9. **Review any custom checklists** (e.g., travel, maintenance).  
10. **Review Someday/Maybe list** – promote viable ideas to projects or delete them.  
11. **Be creative** – add new ideas, opportunities, or courageous goals.  
   - Source: [GTD weekly review checklist](https://gettingthingsdone.com/weekly-review)

## 📅 Monthly
### Migration – *Bullet Journal Monthly Migration* (15‑30 min) 
1. **Open the previous month’s log** and scan every task bullet (`•`).  
2. **Decide for each:**
   - **Complete** → mark `X`.
   - **Delete** → strike through.
   - **Migrate** → turn the bullet into a right‑arrow (`>`).  
3. **Check the Future Log** for events scheduled in the new month; pull them forward.  
4. **Create a new monthly spread:**
   - Left page = calendar dates.
   - Right page = fresh task list.
5. **Copy migrated items** (`>`) onto the new month’s task page.
6. **Add any new monthly goals or themes** you identified during the review.  
   - Source: [Bullet Journal migration guide](https://bulletjournal.com/pages/learn)

## 📆 Quarterly / Seasonal
### Deep‑Thinking Session – *Bill Gates “Think Week”* (1‑2 h) 
1. **Schedule a full week** (or a dedicated 2‑hour block) with no meetings and limited email.  
2. **Disconnect** – turn off notifications, delegate urgent matters.  
3. **Gather reading material** (books, papers, internal memos).  
4. **Read & annotate** – focus on long‑term trends, strategic questions.  
5. **Write a summary** of insights and potential actions.  
6. **Define 2‑3 concrete experiments** to run before the next Think Week.  
   - Source: [Bill Gates Think Week description](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Think-Week) 

### Sprint Review – *12‑Week Year End‑Cycle Review* (45‑60 min) 
1. **Collect performance data** for the past 12 weeks (key metrics, win‑loss).  
2. **Score each goal** against the original target.  
3. **Identify blockers** and root causes.  
4. **Celebrate wins** and note what worked well.  
5. **Define the next 12‑week vision** and set 3‑5 high‑impact goals.  
6. **Create a tactical action list** for the first week of the new cycle.  
   - Source: [12‑Week Year review process](https://the12weekyear.com/annual-review) 

---

## 📅 Yearly
### Annual Review – *Tim Ferriss “Past Year Review”* (2‑4 h) 
1. **Gather quantitative data** (income, hours worked, major projects).  
2. **Perform an 80/20 analysis** – identify the 20 % of activities that produced 80 % of results.  
3. **Run the “Five‑Question” self‑audit:**
   - What did I accomplish?
   - What did I fail to accomplish?
   - What am I grateful for?
   - What lessons did I learn?
   - What will I change next year?
4. **Create a “Future‑Year” vision board** – list top 3‑5 life‑level goals.  
5. **Plan the first quarter** using the same steps as the quarterly review.  
   - Source: [Tim Ferriss annual review guide](https://tim.blog/2020/12/31/annual-review) 

### Yearly Virtues – *Benjamin Franklin’s Yearly Virtue Ledger* (30‑45 min) 
1. **Select 13 virtues** (e.g., Temperance, Silence, Order).  
2. **Score yourself** 1‑9 each day on how well you adhered to each virtue.  
3. **At year‑end, total the scores** and note patterns of strength/weakness.  
4. **Choose one virtue** to focus on for the next year.  
5. **Write a brief commitment statement** for the upcoming year.  
   - Source: [Benjamin Franklin virtue system](https://www.franklinplanner.com/virtues)

## 🧭 Citrine's Built-In Planning & Review Process & Three-Layer Customization Architecture

To support both users who want an anti-overwhelm, science-backed system out of the box and users who bring their own workflow, Citrine implements a **Canonical Multi-Horizon Process** paired with a **Three-Layer Process Customization Engine** and **4-Stage Progressive Learning**.

### 1. Cadence Ritual Structure (Full vs. Minimal / Express Modes)

Every ritual supports a 1-click toggle between **Full Process** (when you have normal capacity) and **⚡ Minimal (Express) Mode** (a 1–3 minute low-energy reset so you never skip a transition out of perfectionism):

- **Daily Morning Plan (`daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`):**
  1. **Scan Today's Hard Landscape** ([GTD Calendar Landscape](https://gettingthingsdone.com/weekly-review)) — inspect time-bound `○` events so you don't over-schedule your free hours. *(Included in Minimal)*
  2. **Triage Yesterday's Carryover** ([Bullet Journal Daily Migration](https://bulletjournal.com/pages/learn)) — decide item-by-item: `Do Today >`, `Schedule 📅`, or `Drop [-]`.
  3. **Lock Today's Top 1–3 Focus Shelf** ([Ivy Lee Method](https://todoist.com/productivity-methods/ivy-lee) & ADHD Working-Memory Cap) — includes a 1-tap **🛟 Rule of 1 (Floor Day)** toggle that caps today's shelf to a single task when energy is low. *(Included in Minimal)*
  4. **Define Your 2-Minute Micro-Step & If-Then Cue** ([Peter Gollwitzer's Implementation Intentions](https://cancercontrol.cancer.gov/brp/research/constructs/implementation-intentions)) — write the first physical 120-second action for #1 plus an `If / When [cue], then I will [action]` anchor.
- **Daily Evening Shutdown (`daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`):**
  1. **Capture Loose Ends & Celebrate Today's Wins** ([Cal Newport's Daily Shutdown](https://focusbox.io/articles/daily-shutdown) & [Benjamin Franklin's Evening Question](https://www.franklinplanner.com/virtues)). *(Included in Minimal)*
  2. **Close or Migrate Today's Open Loops** — reconcile remaining tasks on `daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
  3. **Preview Tomorrow & Pre-Lock Tomorrow's #1 Priority** ([Ivy Lee Next-Day Prep](https://todoist.com/productivity-methods/ivy-lee)) — automatically seeds tomorrow's `daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with your #1 priority. *(Included in Minimal)*
  4. **Declare "Shutdown Complete"** — check the explicit cognitive-offload boundary so your brain stops rehearsing unfinished tasks overnight. *(Included in Minimal)*
- **Weekly Review & Plan (`weekly/YYYY-Www.md`):**
  1. **Celebrate Shipped & Get Clear (Mind-Sweep + Inbox Zero)** ([GTD Weekly Review](https://gettingthingsdone.com/weekly-review)). *(Included in Minimal)*
  2. **Get Current (14-Day Calendar & Waiting-For Follow-Ups)**.
  3. **Project Next-Action Check (Defeat Zombie Projects)**.
  4. **Compassionate 14-Day Stale & Someday/Maybe Triage** — guilt-free archival or promotion.
  5. **Lock 1–3 Weekly Outcomes & Focus Intentions**. *(Included in Minimal)*
- **Monthly Migration & Plan (`monthly/YYYY-MM.md`):**
  1. **Celebrate Monthly Wins & Non-Punitive Habit Rhythm Check-In** ([Bullet Journal Monthly Migration](https://bulletjournal.com/pages/learn)).
  2. **Migrate Previous Month's Open Loops** (`Migrate >`, `Schedule <`, `Drop [-]`). *(Included in Minimal)*
  3. **Pull Forward from Future Log (`future.md`)**. *(Included in Minimal)*
  4. **Set Monthly Theme & Core Goals**. *(Included in Minimal)*
- **Quarterly Review & Plan (`quarterly/YYYY-QN.md`):**
  1. **Score Prior Cycle Goals & Debug Blockers** ([12-Week Year End-Cycle Review](https://the12weekyear.com/annual-review)). *(Included in Minimal)*
  2. **Strategic Synthesis & 1–3 Concrete Experiments** ([Bill Gates Think Week](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Think-Week)).
  3. **Prune Stale Projects & Someday/Maybe**.
  4. **Set 3–5 Quarterly Goals & Seed Week 1 Tactical Action** — automatically bridges your Week 1 action into the current Weekly/Daily log (`QY-04`). *(Included in Minimal)*
- **Yearly Review & Vision (`yearly/YYYY.md`):**
  1. **80/20 Energy Audit (Peak Positive `+` vs. Strict "Not-To-Do" List `-`)** ([Tim Ferriss Past Year Review](https://tim.blog/2020/12/31/annual-review)). *(Included in Minimal)*
  2. **Five-Question Annual Self-Audit** (Accomplished, Failed/Unfinished, Grateful For, Lessons Learned, Change Next Year).
  3. **Focal Virtue / Theme & Buffett 5/25 "Avoid-At-All-Costs" List** ([Benjamin Franklin Virtue System](https://www.franklinplanner.com/virtues)).
  4. **Top 3–5 Annual Goals & Q1 Tactical Bridge**. *(Included in Minimal)*

### 2. Three-Layer Customization Architecture (Bring Your Own Process)

In the **Planning & Review Process Studio** (accessible from `☀️ Today`, `🧭 Horizons & Review`, and `⚙️ Settings`), every cadence ritual can be configured across three layers:

1. **Layer 1 — Built-In Process Presets (`guidedPreset`):** Switch any cadence with 1 click between Citrine's canonical hybrid rituals and classic single-author presets:
   - *Daily Morning*: Citrine Morning Launch, [Ivy Lee 6-Task Method](https://todoist.com/productivity-methods/ivy-lee), [Benjamin Franklin Morning Question](https://www.franklinplanner.com/virtues)
   - *Daily Evening*: Citrine Evening Shutdown, [Cal Newport Shutdown Ritual](https://focusbox.io/articles/daily-shutdown), [Benjamin Franklin Evening Examination](https://www.franklinplanner.com/virtues)
   - *Weekly*: Citrine GTD + CBT Weekly Reset, Classic [GTD Weekly Review](https://gettingthingsdone.com/weekly-review), ADHD 5-Minute Weekly Reset
   - *Monthly*: [Bullet Journal Monthly Migration](https://bulletjournal.com/pages/learn)
   - *Quarterly*: Citrine 12-Week Year + Think Week Hybrid, [Bill Gates Think Week](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Think-Week)
   - *Yearly*: Citrine Yearly Review & Vision, [Tim Ferriss Past Year Review](https://tim.blog/2020/12/31/annual-review), [Benjamin Franklin Yearly Virtue Ledger](https://www.franklinplanner.com/virtues)
2. **Layer 2 — Step Customizer (`customSteps`):** Toggle individual steps on or off, choose which steps appear in **⚡ Minimal (Express)** mode, reorder steps, or add your own custom **Reflection Prompt** or **Checklist** steps (whose responses write directly as clean Markdown headings into the cadence's `.md` file).
3. **Layer 3 — Freeform Markdown (`freeformMarkdown`):** Skip guided modals altogether and open/create the cadence `.md` file directly in the Live Preview editor using your customizable `.citrine/templates/<cadence>.md` template (`B-11`).

### 3. Progressive Learning & 1-Click Opt-Out

- **4 Contextual Learning Stages:** Rather than overwhelming new users with 6 cadences on Day 1, Citrine introduces rituals gradually as they use the app:
  - *Stage 1*: Capture + Morning Top-3 Launch
  - *Stage 2*: Evening Shutdown & Forgiving Daily Migration
  - *Stage 3*: Weekly System Trust Reset
  - *Stage 4*: Monthly, Quarterly & Yearly Horizons
- **Auto-Fading Coach Tips:** Each ritual step displays a *"Why this works & how to avoid overwhelm"* callout during the first 2 completions of that ritual, then automatically collapses into a quiet `?` chip once `completionCount >= 3`.
- **1-Click Opt-Out:** Users who already have their own system can click **Disable Guidance** on `☀️ Today`, inside any ritual, or in `⚙️ Settings` to hide all coaching banners immediately.
