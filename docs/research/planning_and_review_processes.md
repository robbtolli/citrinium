# Planning & Review Processes for Different Cadences

**Goal:** Provide concrete, step‑by‑step routines people use when they sit down to **plan** or **review** their work/life at various intervals. The steps are extracted from well‑known productivity systems and include rough time estimates and typical tools. All items are linked directly to the source material (clickable Markdown links) as you requested.

---

## 📅 Daily
### Planning (5‑15 min) – *Ivy Lee Method* 
1. **Write down 6 tasks** you will tackle tomorrow.  
2. **Rank them** by true importance (1 = most important).  
3. **Start with #1** when you arrive; ignore everything else until it is done.  
4. **Proceed sequentially** through the list, one task at a time.  
5. **Carry over** any unfinished items to the next day’s list (still capped at 6).  
   - Source: [Ivy Lee method explained](https://todoist.com/productivity-methods/ivy-lee) 

### Review (5‑10 min) – *Cal Newport Daily Shutdown* 
1. **Capture loose ends** – move any stray notes or ideas into your master task system.  
2. **Review your master list** for missing items and set due dates if needed.  
3. **Check the next‑day calendar** for upcoming meetings or deadlines.  
4. **Adjust the week’s plan** based on today’s progress.  
5. **Close down** your workspace and say the shutdown phrase (e.g., *“Shutdown complete.”*).  
   - Source: [Newport shutdown routine](https://focusbox.io/articles/daily-shutdown)

## 📆 Weekly
### Planning & Review – *Getting Things Done (GTD) Weekly Review* (30‑60 min) 
1. **Collect & clear all inboxes** (physical papers, email, voice‑mail, digital capture tools).  
2. **Empty your head** – do a mind‑sweep of any lingering ideas.  
3. **Process the inbox** – decide next actions or file as reference.  
4. **Review action lists** – mark completed items, delete outdated ones, add next actions.  
5. **Review past calendar** – note any follow‑ups from recent meetings.  
6. **Review future calendar** – capture actions triggered by upcoming events.  
7. **Review waiting‑for list** – follow up on delegated tasks.  
8. **Review projects** – ensure every project has at least one next action.  
9. **Review any custom checklists** (e.g., travel, maintenance).  
10. **Review Someday/Maybe list** – promote viable ideas to projects or delete them.  
11. **Be creative** – add new ideas, opportunities, or courageous goals.  
   - Source: [GTD weekly review checklist](https://gettingthingsdone.com/weekly-review)

## 📅 Monthly
### Migration – *Bullet Journal Monthly Migration* (15‑30 min) 
1. **Open the previous month’s log** and scan every task bullet (`•`).  
2. **Decide for each:**
   - **Complete** → mark `X`.
   - **Delete** → strike through.
   - **Migrate** → turn the bullet into a right‑arrow (`>`).  
3. **Check the Future Log** for events scheduled in the new month; pull them forward.  
4. **Create a new monthly spread:**
   - Left page = calendar dates.
   - Right page = fresh task list.
5. **Copy migrated items** (`>`) onto the new month’s task page.
6. **Add any new monthly goals or themes** you identified during the review.  
   - Source: [Bullet Journal migration guide](https://bulletjournal.com/pages/learn)

## 📆 Quarterly / Seasonal
### Deep‑Thinking Session – *Bill Gates “Think Week”* (1‑2 h) 
1. **Schedule a full week** (or a dedicated 2‑hour block) with no meetings and limited email.  
2. **Disconnect** – turn off notifications, delegate urgent matters.  
3. **Gather reading material** (books, papers, internal memos).  
4. **Read & annotate** – focus on long‑term trends, strategic questions.  
5. **Write a summary** of insights and potential actions.  
6. **Define 2‑3 concrete experiments** to run before the next Think Week.  
   - Source: [Bill Gates Think Week description](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Think-Week) 

### Sprint Review – *12‑Week Year End‑Cycle Review* (45‑60 min) 
1. **Collect performance data** for the past 12 weeks (key metrics, win‑loss).  
2. **Score each goal** against the original target.  
3. **Identify blockers** and root causes.  
4. **Celebrate wins** and note what worked well.  
5. **Define the next 12‑week vision** and set 3‑5 high‑impact goals.  
6. **Create a tactical action list** for the first week of the new cycle.  
   - Source: [12‑Week Year review process](https://the12weekyear.com/annual-review)

## 📅 Yearly
### Annual Review – *Tim Ferriss “Past Year Review”* (2‑4 h) 
1. **Gather quantitative data** (income, hours worked, major projects).  
2. **Perform an 80/20 analysis** – identify the 20 % of activities that produced 80 % of results.  
3. **Run the “Five‑Question” self‑audit:**
   - What did I accomplish?
   - What did I fail to accomplish?
   - What am I grateful for?
   - What lessons did I learn?
   - What will I change next year?
4. **Create a “Future‑Year” vision board** – list top 3‑5 life‑level goals.  
5. **Plan the first quarter** using the same steps as the quarterly review.  
   - Source: [Tim Ferriss annual review guide](https://tim.blog/2020/12/31/annual-review) 

### Yearly Virtues – *Benjamin Franklin’s Yearly Virtue Ledger* (30‑45 min) 
1. **Select 13 virtues** (e.g., Temperance, Silence, Order).  
2. **Score yourself** 1‑9 each day on how well you adhered to each virtue.  
3. **At year‑end, total the scores** and note patterns of strength/weakness.  
4. **Choose one virtue** to focus on for the next year.  
5. **Write a brief commitment statement** for the upcoming year.  
   - Source: [Benjamin Franklin virtue system](https://www.franklinplanner.com/virtues)
