# Citrinium

Citrinium is an ADHD-friendly desktop app (Windows, Mac, and Linux) and mobile app (Android and iOS) for notes, planning, and task management. It provides a starting point for capturing, clarifying, organizing, reflecting on, and engaging with thoughts, ideas, and tasks, while allowing users to use their own systems and evolve their process and organization over time.

## Goals

- **A Calm, Usable Personal System:** Help people capture notes, ideas, commitments, and tasks; organize them without demanding perfect structure; and turn them into realistic plans and clear next actions. Support calm recovery when plans change, tasks are missed, or a system stops working.
- **ADHD-Friendly Support for Everyone:** Be broadly useful while deliberately reducing executive-function friction. Make memory, time, priorities, and starting work more visible and manageable, with forgiving defaults that reduce blank-page and choice overload.
- **A Guided, Adaptable Starting Point:** Give new users an approachable workflow for daily planning, note-taking, and task management, supported by examples, templates, tutorials, contextual help, and sensible default plugins. Users can keep, modify, replace, or ignore any part of that workflow at any time.
- **Data Ownership and Privacy:** Keep authoritative user data in understandable files on the user's own devices. Core use works offline without a required account or cloud service; any optional sync protects content with end-to-end encryption.
- **Durable User Control:** Keep organization, automation, reminders, views, templates, plugins, and future assistance features understandable, optional, configurable, and reversible. Use portable formats and interoperable designs so Citrinium supports users' evolving systems rather than locking them into one provider or method.

## Guiding Principles

- **Universal utility, ADHD-informed design:** Be broadly useful while treating executive-function barriers as first-class design concerns.
- **Guided start, not forced methodology:** Offer a ready-to-use starting workflow, but never require it.
- **Customization is continuous:** Users can change workflows, organization, defaults, templates, plugins, and views whenever their needs or understanding changes, not only during onboarding.
- **Progressive disclosure:** Begin with a small, understandable set of choices; reveal advanced capabilities when useful.
- **Defaults should teach:** Default notes, planning views, templates, sample content, and enabled plugins should model a coherent starting system without becoming an obligation or adding clutter.
- **Capture now, organize later:** Avoid demanding categories, projects, priorities, or perfect wording while the user is trying to capture something.
- **One item, many useful views:** A note or task may appear in a daily plan, project, search result, review, and calendar without duplicate or conflicting copies.
- **Make the next step visible:** Prefer concrete next actions, context, and start cues over abstract goals and overwhelming backlogs.
- **Make time understandable:** Support commitments, estimates, buffers, reminders, and flexible planning without assigning false precision to every task.
- **Forgiving over punitive:** Missed tasks, skipped routines, and changed plans should lead to calm recovery, not shame, broken streaks, or artificial urgency.
- **User control over automation:** Suggestions, reminders, AI, and social features should be understandable, optional, configurable, and easy to disable.
- **Local-first data ownership:** Device files are the source of truth; indexes and views are rebuildable derivatives.
- **Privacy by design:** Avoid opaque profiling and provider access to content; end-to-end encrypted sync protects content beyond the user's devices.
- **Interoperability and durability:** Use transparent, portable formats and avoid lock-in or irreversible workflows.
- **Help in context:** Offer skippable, contextual guidance alongside durable, deeper documentation.
- **The website is part of the product:** Describe and demonstrate concrete starting workflows and realistic outcomes, rather than overwhelming people with an unlimited feature set.
- **Calm complexity:** Make powerful systems available without making normal use visually or cognitively overwhelming.

## Name
The name Citrinium is a combination of the the Citrine, a gemstone know as "the stone of success", and "-ium" a suffix used for metallic elements, such as Titanium. This app will hopefully help you be more successfully by helping you better manage your notes and track your tasks. The `-ium` ending should invoke an image of your data being stored in a metal vault.
